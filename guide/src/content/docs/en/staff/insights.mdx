Use `인사이트` (Insights) to find students' public GitHub activity and compare department activity and program outcomes. Approved Staff can view this data but cannot change it here.

<Steps>
1. Open ① `인사이트` (Insights) under `교직원` (Staff) in the left menu.
2. In ② the view tabs, choose `순위` (Rankings), `학생 찾기` (Find students), `학과·전공` (Departments and majors), or `프로그램` (Programs).
3. Under ③ `기간` (Period), choose `전체` (All) or a year. This sets the period for rankings, public activity totals, the department activity rate, and total activity per student. `최근 30일 활동` (Last 30 days of activity) uses a separate rolling window. Registration counts, program participation, and program outcomes reflect the current state.
</Steps>

## Find students

<Steps>
1. Open `학생 찾기` (Find students) and enter a name, student number, or GitHub username in ① `검색` (Search).
2. Narrow results by `학과` (Department), `프로그램 참여` (Program participation), `최소 활동량` (Minimum activity), or `최근 30일 활동` (Last 30 days of activity). `미활동` (Inactive) matches only students with a measured zero; choose `전체` (All) to include students with no recent measurement.
3. Check the result count and table. Select `이름` (Name), `학번` (Student number), `활동 합계` (Total activity), `최근 30일 활동` (Last 30 days of activity), or `협업 비율` (Collaboration ratio) to sort. Use `이전` (Previous) and `다음` (Next) if there are multiple pages. Select a name to open the GitHub profile in a new tab.
</Steps>

## Read student rankings

- On `순위` (Rankings), ① `학생 순위` (Student rankings) shows the top 10 students by public activity in the selected period. `활동 합계` (Total activity) includes commits, PRs, issues, repositories, and stars. `최근 30일 활동` (Last 30 days of activity) measures the increase in public activity over that window, excluding stars. Students who also rank among the recent leaders are marked `급상승` (Trending).
- `순위 변화` (Rank change) compares with the previous year. `신규` (New) means the comparison year exists but the student had no rank then. `—` means no year is available for comparison. `협업 비율` (Collaboration ratio) is (PRs + issues) ÷ total activity. When total activity is zero, the ratio is `—`.
- `관심 필요` (Needs attention) lists up to four students who had activity in the selected period but a measured zero in the last 30 days. `이번 달 첫 기여 학생` (Students who first contributed this month) and `마지막 활동` (Last activity) currently show `—` because those values are not calculated.

## Compare department activity

At the top of `학과·전공` (Departments and majors), check `가입 학생` (Registered students), `활동 학생` (Active students), and `학과 수` (Departments). The ① comparison table is ordered by `1인당 활동 합계` (Total activity per student), highest first. It also shows `활동 학생 비율` (Active student rate) and `1인당 최근 30일` (Last 30 days per student). Students without a registered department count toward the student totals above but do not appear in the table. Rows with fewer than three registered students stay in the table and are marked `표본 적음` (Small sample).

## Compare program outcomes

The `프로그램` (Programs) tab shows program count (`운영 프로그램`), approved participants (`승인 참여 학생`, including a distinct count), average competition ratio (`평균 경쟁률`, applications ÷ approvals), and the share of students in two or more programs (`재참여 학생`). `프로그램 성과표` (Program scorecard) shows one row per published program. The period does not apply to this tab.

- `모집` (Recruitment) shows applications, approvals, and the competition ratio. `구성` (Composition) shows the shares of SW majors, non-SW majors, and students with no registered department among approved applicants and team members.
- `완주` (Completion) is the share of required items past their deadline that were submitted, excluding rejected submissions. If no such items exist, the cell shows `—` and `마감된 마일스톤 없음` (No milestones past their deadline).
- `성과` (Outcome) divides commits, PRs, and issues in repositories while linked to approved teams during the program by the program's distinct approved participant count. In-progress programs count activity through now. Releases are excluded. If the value cannot be calculated, the cell shows `—` and `연결된 저장소 없음` (No linked repository).
- Sort by `승인 참여` (Approved participation), `경쟁률` (Competition ratio), `비SW 비율` (Non-SW share), `완주율` (Completion rate), or `성과` (Outcome). Programs without a value sort last.

<Success>
You can see the table and metrics for the selected tab. `0`, `0.0`, and `0%` are measured zeros. `—` means the value is missing or cannot be calculated, not zero.
</Success>

<Recover title="If there are no results or the data cannot load">
- If you see `조건에 맞는 학생이 없습니다` (No students match), try a shorter search term or reset filters to `전체` (All). If rankings are empty, try changing `기간` (Period) to `전체`.
- If the data could not load, select `다시 시도` (Retry). If it still fails, ask an Administrator to check [System status](/oss-hub/en/admin/system-status/).
</Recover>
