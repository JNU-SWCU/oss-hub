`인사이트` (Insights) shows top student activity, department and major figures, and the program participation ranking. Open ① `인사이트` under `교직원` (staff) in the left menu. Only staff and administrators can see it.

<Shot src="staff/insights-full.webp" alt="Insights screen. Marker ① points to Insights in the left menu. The period selector and the Students, Departments and majors, and Programs tabs are at the top, with the selected tab's cards and charts below." caption="① `인사이트` (Insights)" />

## Change the period and tab

<Steps>
1. Select `전체` (All) or a year in ① `기간` (period). It applies to all tabs.
2. Choose the ② `학생` (Students), `학과·전공` (Departments and majors), or `프로그램` (Programs) tab.
3. The cards and charts below update, and a line at the top shows the period and the time the data was captured.
</Steps>
<Shot src="staff/insights-filters.webp" alt="Period and view tabs. Marker ① points to the period selector and ② to the Students, Departments and majors, and Programs view tabs." caption="① `기간` (period) ② view tabs" />

## What each tab shows

- `학생` (Students) tab: choose the `전체` (All) / `SW전공` (SW majors) / `비SW전공` (non-SW majors) filter. On wide screens two cards appear side by side.
  - `<period> 상위 10명` (Top 10 for the period): ranks students by total public activity (Commit, PR, Issue, Repo, Star) in the selected period.
  - `최근 30일 상위 10명` (Top 10, last 30 days): ranks students by the increase in Commit, PR, Issue, and Repo over the rolling last 30 days. Star is excluded, and the from~to dates are shown.
  - Each row shows rank, name (GitHub login if there is no name), department (or `학과 미등록`, department not registered), `@login`, total, and a proportional bar. Student numbers are never shown.
  - Only public activity is counted, so figures can differ from the public ranking page.
- `학과·전공` (Departments and majors) tab: the `SW전공` and `비SW전공` tiles show `가입 학생` (registered students), `활동률` (activity rate: active students / registered students), and `프로그램 참여율` (program participation rate: participating students / registered students). The count of students with `학과 미등록` is shown separately. The department ranking chart below has a toggle between `가입 학생 수` (registered students) and `1인당 활동량` (activity per student). In `1인당 활동량` mode, departments with fewer than 3 students are listed separately as `표본 적음 (가입 3명 미만)` (small sample, fewer than 3 registered).
- `프로그램` (Programs) tab: `프로그램 참여 순위` (program participation ranking). Programs are ranked by approved applicants and team members, counted without duplicates, and stacked as SW majors / non-SW majors / department not registered. The period filter does not apply to this tab.

You can also open a tab by URL: `?view=students`, `?view=groups`, `?view=programs`.

<Success>
The cards and charts reflect your selected period and tab.
</Success>

<Recover title="If the charts are empty or cannot load">
- If nothing matches the selected period the charts can be empty. This is normal. Expand `기간` (period) to `전체` (All).
- If a message says the data could not be loaded, refresh the screen. If it persists, ask an administrator to check [System status](/oss-hub/en/admin/system-status/).
</Recover>
