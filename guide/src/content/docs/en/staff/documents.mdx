Open `서류 현황` (document status) in the program menu to compare teams' submission status across milestones. Select ① a milestone beneath it to see that stage's documents grouped by team. To download individual files or review documents, see [Review submitted documents](/oss-hub/en/staff/review-submission/).

<Shot src="staff/documents-stage-menu.webp" alt="Program left menu with document status selected and its two milestones nested beneath it. Marker ① points to the first milestone." caption="① Choosing a stage narrows the table to it" />

## Read submission status

<Steps>
1. The numbers beside `전체` (all), `미제출` (not submitted), `검토 대기` (awaiting review), `보완 요청` (revision requested), `반려` (rejected), and `승인` (approved) count team-by-milestone cells, not teams. They cover every page of the current search and do not change when you pick a status. Cells marked `—` do not count.
2. On desktop, click `팀` (team), a milestone name, or `진행` (progress) to sort the teams. Click the same heading again to reverse the order. On narrow screens, check the milestones in each team's card.
3. On desktop, click the team name to expand its milestone details. On mobile, these details are already in the team card. Click a linked status to open `최종 제출 검토` (final submission review). To review documents individually, select the milestone name in the left menu.
</Steps>

## Filter teams with missing documents

<Steps>
1. Select `미제출`. On mobile, open the status dropdown. A team stays in the list if any of its milestones is marked `미제출`. To check for missing documents in only one milestone, select that milestone first, then select `미제출`.
2. To find a team, enter its name in the `신청자·팀명·GitHub ID` search box and click `조회` (search). Click `초기화` (reset) to clear both search and status filters.
</Steps>

## Download submissions as a ZIP

<Steps>
1. In the all-milestone `서류 현황` view, click `내려받기` (download) to open `다운로드 범위` (download scope). For `ZIP 내려받기` in the single-milestone view, see [Download milestone materials](/oss-hub/en/staff/review-submission/#download-milestone-materials).
2. Choose `프로그램 전체` (whole program), ① `마일스톤` (milestone), or `팀` (team). For milestone or team scope, select the target.
3. In `ZIP 폴더 묶기` (ZIP folder grouping), select `팀별로 묶기` (group by team) or `서류별로 묶기` (group by document).
4. Click `ZIP 내려받기` (download ZIP).
</Steps>
<Shot src="staff/documents-download.webp" alt="Download scope panel with whole-program, milestone, and team choices, ZIP grouping, and download ZIP. Marker ① points to milestone scope." caption="① `마일스톤` (milestone) download scope" />

The ZIP includes current submission text and files from approved teams in the chosen scope. The list's search and status filters do not affect the ZIP. Earlier submissions are not included.

## Send revision requests in bulk

Students receiving a revision request check its reason and the submission screen instructions before resubmitting. Your reason is shown exactly as written, so be specific about what needs changing.

<Steps>
1. Click `보완 요청 보내기` (send revision requests).
2. Select `프로그램 전체` or `팀` as the scope. To include submissions already approved or rejected, enable `이미 승인·반려한 제출도 포함` (include previously approved or rejected submissions).
3. Check the target count.
4. Optionally enter up to 2,000 characters in `사유 (선택)` (reason, optional). You can leave it blank; there is no separate deadline field.
5. Click `{n}건 보완 요청 보내기` (send revision requests for the shown number of submissions).
</Steps>
<Shot src="staff/requirement-bulk-review.webp" alt="Bulk revision request panel. Marker ② points to scope, ③ to the target count, and ⑤ to the send button. The reason is optional and there is no deadline field." caption="② Scope ③ Target count ⑤ Send" />

Requests reach students immediately. Check the target count before sending.

<Success>
`제출 {n}건에 보완 요청을 보냈습니다.` confirms how many revision requests were sent. For a ZIP, `ZIP 파일을 준비했습니다. 브라우저의 다운로드 목록에서 확인해 주세요.` says it is ready; find it in your browser's downloads list.
</Success>

<Recover title="If the table is empty">
- `마일스톤이 없습니다` means the program has no milestones. Add one in [Edit program dates and submission items](/oss-hub/en/staff/edit-program/).
- `참여 중인 신청이 없습니다` means there are no approved teams yet. See [Approve or reject team applications](/oss-hub/en/staff/decide-application/).
- `조건에 맞는 팀이 없습니다` or `검색 결과가 없습니다` means no teams match the filter or search. Click `필터 초기화` (reset filters).
- If the revision request target count is zero, change the scope or enable `이미 승인·반려한 제출도 포함`.
</Recover>
