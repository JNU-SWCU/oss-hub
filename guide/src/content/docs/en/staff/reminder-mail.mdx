Choose a custom message when you want to pick the recipients and write the subject. Choose a deadline reminder when you want students to see what they still owe and staff to receive a summary.

## Open the mail popup

Click ① `메일 보내기` (send mail) at the top right of the program overview. The same button sits under `제출 마감 알림` (submission deadline reminders) in the `운영 설정` (operating settings) section of the editor. It appears only for accounts whose member type is `교직원` (staff).

<Shot src="staff/mail-entry.webp" alt="Separate custom mail and deadline reminder buttons in the program overview." caption="① Custom mail ② Deadline reminders" />

## Choose the recipients

The popup opens on `받는 학생` (student recipients). Each team that applied is listed with one of `승인` (approved), `검토 대기` (awaiting review), or `반려` (rejected). Rejected teams can be emailed too.

<Steps>
1. Click ① `24시간 이내 필수 서류 미제출자 선택` (select students who have not submitted required documents due within 24 hours). It selects students only from approved teams that still owe a required document due within 24 hours.
2. ② Click the box to the left of a team name to select the whole team. A team with only some members selected shows a mixed box.
3. Use the box next to a student's name to add or remove that person. The count appears as `N명 선택` (N selected) at the top right.
</Steps>

<Shot src="staff/mail-compose.webp" alt="The student recipient area of the mail popup. Each team carries a rejected, approved, or awaiting review status. ① points at the quick-select button and ② frames one team." caption="① Quick selection ② Team selection and the unavailable row" />

`전체 선택` (select all) selects eligible students and `선택 해제` (clear selection) removes them all. A student marked `메일 수신 불가` (cannot receive mail) cannot be selected: they turned `마감 임박 알림 받기` (receive deadline reminders) off in their settings, have no recipient address, or their account is inactive. A program with no applications shows `신청 학생이 없습니다.` (no students have applied). If you try to select more than 200 students, `수신자는 최대 200명까지 선택할 수 있습니다.` (maximum 200 recipients) appears and you cannot preview or send until you reduce the selection.

On a narrow screen the popup fills most of the window and the buttons stack one per row. Selection works the same way.

<Shot src="staff/mail-mobile.webp" alt="The mail popup on a narrow screen. Buttons are stacked vertically, team cards follow below, and cancel and preview buttons sit at the bottom." caption="The recipient area on a narrow screen" />

## Write the subject and the body

Write the message in `메일 내용` (message).

<Steps>
1. Write ① `제목` (subject). It allows up to 200 characters, and the count appears under the field.
2. Write ② `본문` (body). It allows up to 4,000 characters and keeps your line breaks.
3. Once recipients, subject, and body are all present, ③ `미리보기` (preview) becomes available.
</Steps>

<Shot src="staff/mail-content.webp" alt="The message area at the bottom of the mail popup. ① points at the subject field, ② at the body field, and ③ at the preview button." caption="① Subject ② Body ③ `미리보기` (preview)" />

## Check the preview and send

`미리보기` turns the same popup into the recipient list and the message as it will arrive.

<Steps>
1. Read the names under `받는 학생 N명` (N student recipients) and confirm they are the people you mean.
2. Check `프로그램` (program), `보낸 사람` (sender), and `제목` (subject). `[oss-hub]` is added to the front of the subject automatically.
3. Check the body and the notice below it, then click ① `보내기` (send). Use `수정` (edit) to go back and change something.
</Steps>

<Shot src="staff/mail-preview.webp" alt="The preview step of the mail popup. It shows the recipient count and names, the program, sender, and subject, the body, and the notice that sent mail cannot be recalled, with ① pointing at the send button." caption="① `보내기` (send)" />

Below the body sits `이 메일은 oss-hub에서 발송했습니다.` (this mail was sent from oss-hub), which recipients also see, and then `보낸 메일은 취소할 수 없습니다.` (sent mail cannot be recalled). The mail a student receives opens with `안녕하세요, 이름님.` (hello, name) and names the program and the sender. Nothing can undo a send, so read the recipient count and the body once more first. A preview is valid for 10 minutes, and changing the recipients or the message means previewing again.

## Check the result after closing the popup

<Steps>
1. In the same program, reopen `메일 보내기` (Send mail) and choose ① `발송 기록` (Send history) next to the popup title.
2. Select ② the send time, shown in Korea Standard Time. To look for an older send, choose `이전 기록` (Older records).
</Steps>

<Shot src="staff/mail-history-entry.webp" alt="① marks Send history next to the Send mail popup title." caption="① Open send history" />
<Shot src="staff/mail-history.webp" alt="② marks a record labeled with its date and time in the send history list." caption="② Select the send time" />

Opening a record does not resend the message.

<Success>
When processing finishes, the result shows `발송` (Sent), `실패` (Failed), `확인 불가` (Unconfirmed), and `중복 제외` (Duplicates skipped). If any sends were cancelled, `취소 N명` (N cancelled) also appears. Unconfirmed means the system could not verify whether the message was sent. Since it may already have arrived, the system never resends to these recipients. Check with the student directly if needed. If you see `메일 발송을 처리 중입니다.` (Sending is in progress), it continues even if you choose `닫기` (Close). Open the same record later to check the outcome.
</Success>

<Recover title="If the mail cannot be sent">
A failure shows a `메일을 처리하지 못했습니다` (the mail could not be processed) alert in the popup with the reason below it.

- `메일 수신자를 불러오지 못했습니다` (could not load recipients): click `다시 시도` (retry).
- If `발송 기록을 불러오지 못했습니다` (could not load send history) or `발송 결과를 불러오지 못했습니다` (could not load the result) appears, choose `다시 시도` (Retry) to look it up rather than sending again. If you see `발송 기록이 없습니다.` (No send records), check that you opened the same program.
- If the preview expires or the recipients change, click `미리보기` (preview) again before sending.
- `메일 발송이 일시 중지되었습니다. 잠시 후 다시 확인하세요.` (sending is paused, check again later): sending is temporarily stopped. Wait and try again.
- `발송 기록에서 결과를 확인하세요.` (check the result in send history): the request was accepted but the result did not come back right away. Don't send again; open that send in `발송 기록` (Send history).
- `메일을 연속으로 보낼 수 없습니다. 잠시 후 다시 시도해 주세요.` (you cannot send repeatedly): within 10 minutes one person can send three times and one program five times. Wait and send again.
- `한 번에 선택할 수 있는 학생 수를 초과했습니다.` (too many students selected): split the send into groups of 200 or fewer.
- `선택한 학생이 이 프로그램의 메일 발송 대상이 아닙니다.` (a selected student is not a recipient for this program): close the popup, open it again, and choose the recipients. A student's notification setting or account status changed while you were choosing.
</Recover>

## Send deadline reminders and a staff summary

Choose ② `마감 알림 보내기` (send deadline reminders) on the program overview, or ① in the editor’s `운영 설정` (operating settings). Unlike a custom message, a reminder goes to eligible students who still owe required documents due within 24 hours. Eligible staff and administrators receive a summary.

<Shot src="staff/deadline-settings.webp" alt="The editor shows two separate mail actions below the submission deadline reminder setting." caption="① Open deadline reminders from the editor" />

<Steps>
1. To add a note, use ① `학생용 추가 안내` (note to students) or ② `교직원용 추가 안내` (note to staff). Each is optional and allows up to 4,000 characters.
2. Choose ③ `미리보기` (preview).
</Steps>

<Shot src="staff/deadline-compose.webp" alt="The reminder dialog has separate note fields for the two audiences." caption="① Student note ② Staff note ③ Preview" />

Read the student count and `교직원 요약 수신` (staff summary recipients) under ① `발송 대상` (recipients). Use `미리 볼 학생` (student to preview) to inspect an individual reminder alongside the staff summary. On narrow screens, switch between the `학생용` (student) and `교직원용` (staff) tabs.

<Shot src="staff/deadline-preview.webp" alt="Recipient counts above the student reminder and staff summary previews, with the send button at the bottom." caption="① Recipients ② Send" />

Read the final line, `학생 N명 · 교직원 N명 · 발송 후 취소 불가` (N students · N staff · cannot be recalled). Choose ② `보내기` (send) only when you are ready to send both messages. To change a note, choose `수정` (edit).

<Success>`발송 결과` (Send result) is split into `학생` (Students) and `교직원` (Staff). Each shows `발송` (Sent), `실패` (Failed), `확인 불가` (Unconfirmed), `중복 제외` (Duplicates skipped), and `취소` (Cancelled) counts. Deadline reminders go through the same sending path as regular mail, so after closing the dialog you can see the same result in `발송 기록` (Send history) under `메일 보내기` (Send mail). Unconfirmed recipients are never sent to again.</Success>

<Recover title="If you cannot send deadline reminders">
- If the button is replaced by `제출 마감 알림을 켜야 합니다.` (enable submission deadline reminders), turn on `제출 마감 알림` in the editor and save. The separate `메일 보내기` action is still available.
- A preview is valid for 10 minutes. If it expires or the recipients change, click `미리보기` (preview) again before sending. If neither group has recipients, the send button is unavailable.
</Recover>

## Automatic deadline reminders

When a program has `제출 마감 알림` (submission deadline reminders) enabled, the system sends email every day at 9 a.m. Korea Standard Time under these rules.

- A milestone has required documents due within 24 hours.
- Only students who have not submitted those documents receive the email.
- One person does not receive two messages for the same program on the same day.
- The automatic reminder does not include a staff summary.

Automatic subjects also begin with `[oss-hub]`. For one milestone the subject carries the remaining time and the name, such as `[oss-hub] 제출 마감 24시간 전 · 마일스톤 이름` (24 hours until submission deadline · milestone name); for several it becomes `마감 임박 제출 N건` (N submissions due soon). The body lists the program, milestone, and deadline, and carries a `제출하러 가기` (go to submission) button.

To turn reminders off, clear `제출 마감 알림` in the operating settings during [program creation](/oss-hub/en/staff/create-program/) or in the `운영 설정` section of the editor. Turning them off does not affect sending mail yourself with `메일 보내기`.
