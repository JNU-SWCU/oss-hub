The `교직원` (staff) role creates programs, decides student team applications, and reviews submitted documents in OSS Hub. After signing up, you must wait for an administrator to approve your role.

## What staff do

| Task | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Request the role and wait for approval | Sign-up step 2 and the approval waiting screen |
| Check operations | `운영 대시보드` (operations dashboard) |
| Create and edit programs | `프로그램 만들기` (create program) and the program editor |
| Decide team applications | The program's `팀 관리` (team management) |
| Track and review documents | `서류 현황` (document status) and `서류 수합` (document collection) in `프로그램 개요` (program overview) |
| Publish announcements | The program's `게시판` (board) |
| Compare student engagement | `학생 활성` (student engagement) |
| Send deadline reminders | `제출 마감 알림` (submission deadline reminders) in the program editor |

## Get started in five minutes

<Steps>
1. Sign in with GitHub and select `교직원` in sign-up step 2. See [Sign up as staff and wait for approval](/oss-hub/en/staff/first-entry/).
2. Once an administrator approves you, `운영 대시보드` opens. Get familiar with it in [See your programs at a glance](/oss-hub/en/staff/dashboard/).
3. Use `프로그램 만들기` to [create a program](/oss-hub/en/staff/create-program/), then [add submission items](/oss-hub/en/staff/edit-program/) in the editor.
4. When students apply, [approve or reject their applications](/oss-hub/en/staff/decide-application/) in `팀 관리`.
5. When documents arrive, [review them](/oss-hub/en/staff/review-submission/) in `서류 수합`.
</Steps>

## Common points of confusion

- `가입 신청` in the left menu is the queue for staff role requests. Decide student program applications in the program's `팀 관리`. See [Handle staff role requests](/oss-hub/en/admin/applicants/) for the role-request queue.
- Decide applications using the status selector in the list. Changing `검토 대기` (awaiting review) to `승인` (approved) takes effect without a confirmation dialog.
- You cannot decide an application for a team you belong to. You can select a result, but saving it is refused.
- Once a repository has been created, you cannot change approval to rejection.
- Students receive decisions as notices at the top of their dashboard, not by email.
- Only an `관리자` (administrator) can reactivate an account. See [Deactivate and reactivate accounts](/oss-hub/en/admin/deactivate/).
