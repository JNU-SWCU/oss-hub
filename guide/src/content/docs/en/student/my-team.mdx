## Check the dashboard

Click `대시보드` (Dashboard) at the top to open `내 대시보드` (My dashboard). Programs you applied to appear as cards, each showing the next submission deadline, document progress, new feedback, and repository status.

<Shot src="student/dashboard.webp" alt="My dashboard, with one in-progress card and one application-status card. ① points to Our team on the first card." caption="① Our team" />

- Use the chips at the top: `전체` (All), `진행 중` (In progress), `새 피드백 있음` (New feedback), `마친 프로그램` (Completed programs), and `신청 상태` (Application status). The number is the count of cards in that group. A chip appears only when it has cards and its count is smaller than the total.
- Before approval, applications appear under `신청 상태` (Application status) with a `신청` (Applied) badge; rejected applications show `반려` (Rejected). Open `신청 상세` (Application details) for the application screen or `우리 팀` (Our team) for the team screen.
- Card links `제출 현황` (Submission status), `서류 내기` (Submit documents), and `다시 내기` (Resubmit) lead to [document submission](/oss-hub/en/student/submit/).
- If you have not applied to any programs, `아직 신청한 프로그램이 없습니다` (You have not applied to any programs yet) and `프로그램 둘러보기` (Browse programs) appear.

## Approval and rejection results

A notification appears on the dashboard when a result is available. `교직원` (staff) decide whether to approve or reject applications.

| Notification | What to do next |
| --- | --- |
| `{프로그램} 신청이 승인되었습니다` (Your program application was approved) | Click `제출 일정 확인` (Check submission schedule) to see [document deadlines](/oss-hub/en/student/submit/). If the program provisions repositories, check your [repository](/oss-hub/en/student/repository/) too. |
| `{프로그램} 신청이 반려되었습니다` (Your program application was rejected) | Click `반려 사유 확인` (Check reason for rejection) to see `반려 사유` (Reason for rejection) in the application screen. The same reason appears in `우리 팀` (Our team). If no reason was provided, Our team shows `교직원이 남긴 사유가 없습니다. 자세한 내용은 주관 부서에 문의해 주세요.` (Staff did not leave a reason. Contact the organizing department for details). |
| `{프로그램} 신청이 다시 검토 대기로 돌아갔습니다` (Your program application is awaiting review again) | Staff reversed the decision. Click `신청 상태 확인` (Check application status) to open the application and wait for another review. |

<Shot src="student/notice-approved.webp" alt="Approval notification on the dashboard. ① points to Check submission schedule beside the approval message." caption="① Check submission schedule" />
<Shot src="student/notice-rejected.webp" alt="Rejection notification on the dashboard. ① points to Check reason for rejection beside the rejection message." caption="① Check reason for rejection" />

Application results are not emailed. Check these notifications and `우리 팀` (Our team). Email only reminds you about approaching deadlines. See [Settings](/oss-hub/en/student/settings/) for details.

## Apply again after rejection

During the application period, the team leader can edit and resubmit a rejected application. The team and members stay the same; staff do not need to return it to awaiting review first. <Verified status="policy" />

<Steps>
1. Read the reason for rejection in `우리 팀` (Our team). If no reason was provided, ask the organizing department what needs to change.
2. Click `신청서 확인·수정` (View or edit application) to open the application.
3. Revise it to address the reason.
4. Click `신청서 다시 제출` (Resubmit application), then confirm with `다시 제출` (Resubmit) in the dialog.
</Steps>

<Success>
The process is complete when `신청서를 다시 제출했습니다` (Application resubmitted) appears. The application returns to `검토 대기` (Awaiting review) for a staff decision.
</Success>

To apply with another team, leave your team instead of resubmitting. Leaders use `신청 취소` (Cancel application) to cancel the current application, then apply again. Invited students must accept again to join the new team. Cancellation is not possible if there are submission records or a linked repository. Members can use `팀 탈퇴` (Leave team) and join another team.

<Recover title="When the application period has ended">
After the application period, the screen shows `신청 기간이 아닙니다` (Outside the application period). You cannot resubmit, cancel, or submit a new application. Contact the organizing department. Staff can change a rejected application directly to `승인` (Approved).
</Recover>

## Cancel an application

During the application period, the current team leader can cancel a team application awaiting review or rejected. Cancellation is not possible if there are submission records or a linked repository.

Approved applications cannot be edited or canceled. You also cannot cancel outside the application period or if you are not the current team leader.

<Steps>
1. Under `신청 상태` (Application status) in `우리 팀` (Our team), click ① `신청서 확인·수정` (View or edit application). `신청 상세` (Application details) under `신청 상태` (Application status) on the dashboard opens the same screen.
2. Click `신청 취소` (Cancel application) at the bottom of the application screen.
3. In `신청을 취소하시겠습니까?` (Cancel this application?), click `신청 취소` (Cancel application) to return to `대시보드` (Dashboard). The cancelled team and application no longer appear in lists, memberships are removed, and sent invitations are cleared. During the application period, you can create a new team and submit again.
</Steps>

## The Our team screen

Click `우리 팀` (Our team) on a dashboard card to open the team's page for that program. From the top, it shows the team name, `신청 상태` (Application status), and `팀 구성원` (Team members). After approval, `프로젝트 저장소` (Project repository), `우리 팀 활동` (Our team activity), and `제출 현황` (Submission status) follow. `팀 관리` (Team management) is at the bottom.

### Leave a team

A leader with other members remaining must transfer leadership first. In `팀 구성원` (Team members), click the ② arrow icon on the chosen member's row and confirm with `팀장 위임` (Transfer leadership). Hovering displays `{이름} 팀장 위임` (Transfer leadership to this person). The next leader is not selected automatically.

After the transfer, you remain a regular member. Click `팀 탈퇴` (Leave team) in `팀 관리` (Team management) and confirm to leave. If other members remain, the team's application and submission records are retained.

To remove a specific member, the leader clicks the icon at the end of that member's row. Hovering displays `팀에서 제외` (Remove from team).

If only one member remains, the button changes to `팀 삭제` (Delete team).

- Before approval, if there are no submission records or linked repositories, the team and application no longer appear in lists after the last member leaves. Sent invitations are also cleared.
- The last member of an approved team cannot leave.
- The last member of a team with submission records or a linked repository also cannot leave.

If `교직원` (staff) delete the team, the dashboard shows `{프로그램}의 팀 ‘{팀}’이 삭제되었습니다` (This team in the program has been deleted). The deletion reason appears with it; dismiss it with `확인했어요` (Got it). This notification is not sent when you leave and the team and application no longer appear in lists.

<Success>
After approval, the card moves to `진행 중` (In progress), and `제출 현황` (Submission status) appears in `우리 팀` (Our team).
</Success>

<Recover title="When a status looks unexpected">
- If a dashboard card is missing, check that the top chip is `전체` (All).
- `팀장만 다른 팀원을 제외할 수 있습니다.` (Only the team leader can remove other members): members cannot use this feature.
- If a result takes a long time, the application is still waiting for `교직원` (staff) review. For urgent questions, use the program board or contact the organizing department.
</Recover>
