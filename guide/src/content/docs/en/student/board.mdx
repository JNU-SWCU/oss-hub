Each program has its own board, available only to approved participants and `교직원` (staff). Staff post notices; `학생` (students) post questions.

## Post a question

<Steps>
1. Open `게시판` (Board) in the left menu of the program screen.
2. Click ① `질문 쓰기` (Write a question).
3. Fill in `제목` (Title) and `내용` (Content), then click ① `올리기` (Post).
4. Click a post in the list to read it and its comments. To comment, enter your text in ① the field, then click ② `댓글 달기` (Add comment).
</Steps>

The list's `구분` (Type) is either `공지` (Notice) or `질문` (Question), and `작성자` (Author) appears as `이름 · 역할` (Name · Role). Your own posts show ① `수정` (Edit) and ② `삭제` (Delete); comments have only `삭제` (Delete). Clicking Delete opens a confirmation dialog. Confirm to delete.

<Shot src="student/board-post-own.webp" alt="Your own question post. ① points to Edit and ② to Delete, with the comments area below." caption="① Edit  ② Delete" />

<Success>
Your post appears in the list. `교직원` (staff) reply in the comments.
</Success>

<Recover title="When you cannot open the board">
- If `아직 참여자가 아닙니다` (You are not a participant yet) appears, click ① `신청하러 가기` (Go to apply), or return with `프로그램 개요로` (Back to program overview). Before approval, `게시판` (Board) is hidden from the left menu and cannot be used even by opening its address directly.
</Recover>
