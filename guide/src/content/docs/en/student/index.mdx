The `학생` (student) role lets you find programs in OSS Hub, apply as a team, and use repositories and submit documents after approval. Activity records accumulate on the dashboard and in the rankings.

## What students do

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Sign up and complete your profile | Three-step flow after your first login |
| Find a program | `프로그램` (Programs) list and program details |
| Create a team and apply | The program application screen |
| Select teammates, send more invitations, and accept invitations | `팀원 지정` (Select teammates) in the application, `우리 팀` (Our team) after submission, and `팀 초대 알림` (Team invitations) at the top |
| Check or link a repository | `내 저장소` (My repositories), or the program's `우리 팀` (Our team) |
| Submit and resubmit documents | `마일스톤` (Milestones) and `제출 현황` (Submission status) in program details |
| Use the board | The program's `게시판` (Board) |
| View activity and rankings | `내 활동` (My activity) and `랭킹` (Rankings) |

## Get started in five minutes

<Steps>
1. Click `로그인` (Log in) at the top right and sign in with GitHub.
2. Complete the consent, role (`학생`, student), and basic information steps. See [Sign up and complete your profile](/oss-hub/en/student/sign-up/).
3. In `프로그램` (Programs), choose a program marked `모집중` (Accepting applications) and [apply](/oss-hub/en/student/apply/).
4. After approval, check your next deadline in `내 대시보드` (My dashboard) and [submit your documents](/oss-hub/en/student/submit/).
</Steps>

## Common points of confusion

- Teams are created in the application screen. There is no separate team creation screen or join code.
- `교직원` (staff) approve applications and review documents. Their screens are explained in the [Staff guide](/oss-hub/en/staff/).
- `관리자` (administrators) reactivate accounts. See [Deactivate and reactivate accounts](/oss-hub/en/admin/deactivate/).
