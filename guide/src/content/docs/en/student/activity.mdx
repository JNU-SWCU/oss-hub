Activity is collected automatically from your team repository. Collection starts after the repository is linked, so your [repository](/oss-hub/en/student/repository/) must be ready first.

## My activity

<Steps>
1. Open `내 활동` (My activity) in the left menu. This screen is available only to students.
2. Choose a time unit using the `월별` (Monthly) or `연도별` (Yearly) chip.
3. Under `참여 프로그램` (Participating programs), see the programs you joined by year. Under `활동 추이` (Activity trends), see how your activity changes over time.
</Steps>

The activity total here adds Commit, Pull Request, and Release; Issue is counted separately. This differs from the ranking total. Team-level activity is also shown under `우리 팀 활동` (Our team activity) in `우리 팀` (Our team).

## Rankings

You can open `랭킹` (Rankings) at the top without signing in. Rankings total Commit, PR, Issue, Repo, and Star. Star combines two counts: stars on your own public repositories, and stars on your organization's public repositories to which you personally committed or submitted a PR that year.

In the `순위 변화` (Ranking changes) chart, each of the top ten participants has a different colored line with a profile photo at its end. Other participants appear as faint gray lines. Hover over a name in the legend to emphasize that person's line; click it to hide or show the line and profile photo.

Repository activity appears as charts and numbers. Updates may take time to appear.
</Success>

<Recover title="When activity is empty">
- Activity is empty if no repository is linked or collection has not run yet. Check [repository registration](/oss-hub/en/student/repository/).
- Activity also appears empty if the repository has no commits yet.
</Recover>
