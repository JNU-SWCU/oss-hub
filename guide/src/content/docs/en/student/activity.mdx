Activity is collected automatically from your team repository. Collection starts after the repository is linked, so your [repository](/oss-hub/en/student/repository/) must be ready first.

## My activity

<Steps>
1. Open `내 활동` (My activity) in the left menu. This screen is available only to students.
2. Choose a time unit using the `월별` (Monthly) or `연도별` (Yearly) chip.
3. Under `참여 프로그램` (Participating programs), see the programs you joined by year. Under `활동 추이` (Activity trends), see how your activity changes over time.
</Steps>

## Program activity

Under `활동 현황` (Activity) on the program details page, `Commit`, `PR`, and `Release` appear in separate charts. Click ① the team's `팀원별 기여` (Member contributions) row to expand the member totals; click again to collapse them. Check `데이터 기준` (Data as of) below for the collection time.

<Shot src="student/program-activity.webp" alt="Program activity with separate Commit, PR, and Release charts and expanded member totals below. ① points to the expand button." caption="① Expand Member contributions to check each member's totals." />

Teams without a repository or collected activity show a reason instead of a chart. If collection fails, check the notice and last collection time, then try again later.

## Rankings

You can open `랭킹` (Rankings) at the top without signing in. Rankings total commits, PRs, issues, repositories and stars. The tooltip beside the title says `스타는 직접 커밋·PR한 공개 저장소의 것만 인정됩니다.` — only stars on public repositories you committed to or raised a PR on are counted.

Choose the period in the left list. It opens on `전체` (all time) and adds up every score collected so far. Below it, the current year comes first, then earlier years. Choosing a year counts January to December of that year only. The chosen period stays in the address, so browser back and forward move between periods.

The `순위 변화` (Ranking changes) chart follows the chosen period: `전체` groups the whole collected span, and a year shows that year month by month. Each of the top ten participants has a different colored line with a profile photo at its end. Other participants appear as faint gray lines. Hover over a name in the legend to emphasize that person's line; click it to hide or show the line and profile photo.
