## Update your profile and notifications

<Steps>
1. Open `설정` (Settings) from the account menu at the top right.
2. Update your name, phone number, and affiliation in your profile. Students cannot change their student number. The screen also says `학번은 변경할 수 없습니다.` (Your student number cannot be changed).
3. Under `알림 수신` (Notifications), check `수신 이메일` (Recipient email) and turn on `마감 임박 알림 받기` (Receive approaching-deadline reminders) if you want them. It is off by default.
4. Click `저장` (Save) at the bottom. `저장되었습니다.` (Saved) appears.
</Steps>

OSS Hub emails only reminders about approaching deadlines, and only to people who enable `마감 임박 알림 받기` (Receive approaching-deadline reminders). If you entered the wrong student number, contact an `관리자` (administrator). The correction process is not explained on screen. <Verified status="policy" />

## Deactivate your account

Deactivation logs you out immediately. You cannot use the service even if you sign in again. Only an `관리자` (administrator) can reactivate your account. Submission records and consent history are not deleted.

<Steps>
1. In `설정` (Settings), find `계정 관리` (Account management) and click `계정 비활성화` (Deactivate account).
2. Read the notice in the `계정을 비활성화하시겠습니까?` (Deactivate your account?) dialog.
3. Click ① `비활성화하고 로그아웃` (Deactivate and log out).
</Steps>

<Success>
The `계정이 비활성화되었습니다` (Account deactivated) screen appears. Use `홈으로 돌아가기` (Return home) to leave it.
</Success>

<Recover title="If you want to use your account again">
Ask an `관리자` (administrator) to reactivate it. See the [Administrator guide](/oss-hub/en/admin/deactivate/) for the process.
</Recover>
