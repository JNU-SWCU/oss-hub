## How a program works

1. **Explore.** Check application dates, program dates, and required submissions.
2. **Form a team and apply.** Choose a team name, invite teammates, and submit your application.
3. **Receive a decision.** Staff approve or reject the application.
4. **Work, submit, and review.** Approved teams submit documents for review and revise them when changes are requested.

<Shot src="landing/hero.webp" alt="Staff dashboard showing counts of programs accepting applications, in progress, and ended. The product interface is in Korean." caption="The staff dashboard brings program operations together." />

Application approval and document review are separate. Staff and administrators cannot decide applications or review submissions for their own team.
