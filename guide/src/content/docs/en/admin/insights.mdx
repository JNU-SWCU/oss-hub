`인사이트` (Insights) shows top student activity, department and major figures, and the program scorecard. Use it to explain the initiative's impact or identify groups with lower participation. Approved staff can open it; it does not change data. Accounts with administrator access alone do not see it.

<Steps>
1. Open ① `인사이트` (Insights) in the left-hand menu.
2. Under ① `기간` (Period), choose `전체` (All) or a year. It applies to the `학생` (Students) tab rankings and to activity numbers (`활동률`, activity rate, and `1인당 활동량`, activity per student). Participation numbers (`프로그램 참여율` on the `학과·전공` tab, and the `프로그램` tab) show the current approved state regardless of the period.
3. Choose the ② `학생` (Students), `학과·전공` (Departments and majors), or `프로그램` (Programs) tab.
</Steps>

## What each tab shows

- `학생` (Students) tab: choose the `전체` (All) / `SW전공` (SW majors) / `비SW전공` (non-SW majors) filter. On wide screens two cards appear side by side.
  - `<period> 상위 10명` (Top 10 for the period): ranks students by total public activity (Commit, PR, Issue, Repo, Star) in the selected period.
  - `최근 30일 상위 10명` (Top 10, last 30 days): ranks students by the increase in Commit, PR, Issue, and Repo over the rolling last 30 days. Star is excluded, and the from~to dates are shown.
  - Each row shows rank, name (GitHub login if there is no name), department (or `학과 미등록`, department not registered), `@login`, total, and a proportional bar. Student numbers are never shown.
  - Only public activity is counted, so figures can differ from the public ranking page.
- `학과·전공` (Departments and majors) tab: the `SW전공` and `비SW전공` tiles show `가입 학생` (registered students), `활동률` (activity rate: active students / registered students), and `프로그램 참여율` (program participation rate: participating students / registered students). The `프로그램 참여율` tiles carry the note `현재 승인 기준 · 기간 미적용` (current approved state, period not applied) because they count current approved participation regardless of the period. The count of students with `학과 미등록` is shown separately. The department ranking chart below has a toggle between `가입 학생 수` (registered students) and `1인당 활동량` (activity per student). In `1인당 활동량` mode, departments with fewer than 3 students are listed separately as `표본 적음 (가입 3명 미만)` (small sample, fewer than 3 registered).
- `프로그램` (Programs) tab: tiles at the top show `운영 프로그램` (programs), `승인 참여 학생` (approved students, with the de-duplicated count), `평균 경쟁률` (average competition ratio, applications ÷ approvals), and `재참여 학생` (share of students in two or more programs). Below, `프로그램 성과표` (program scorecard) compares each published program on one row: `모집` (recruitment: applications → approvals and the ratio), `구성` (composition: SW majors / non-SW majors / department not registered among approved applicants and team members), `완주` (completion: share of required items past their deadline that were submitted, rejected ones excluded), and `성과` (outcome: Commit, PR, and Issue in approved teams’ repositories from the program start until the earlier of its end or now, only while the repository was linked to that team, divided by the program’s de-duplicated approved participant count; Release is excluded, and this is not a comparison with the preceding 30 days because events exist only for tracked team repositories and earlier student activity is a yearly cumulative snapshot). When a value cannot be computed, `—` appears with the reason (`마감된 마일스톤 없음`, no milestone past its deadline; `연결된 저장소 없음`, no linked repository). Sort with the `승인 참여`, `경쟁률`, `비SW 비율`, `완주율`, and `성과` buttons under the column titles; programs without a value always sort last. The period filter does not apply to this tab.

You can also open a tab by URL: `?view=students`, `?view=groups`, `?view=programs`.

<Success>
Numbers and charts update for the selected period and tab, with a line at the top showing the period and the time the data was captured.
</Success>

<Recover title="If the screen is empty or cannot load">
If there is nothing to aggregate for the selected period the screen can be empty. Expand `기간` (Period) to `전체` (All). If a message says the data could not be loaded, refresh the page.
</Recover>
