`학생 활성` (Student engagement) compares GitHub activity and program participation by major. Use it to explain the initiative's impact or identify groups with lower participation. Approved staff can open it; it does not change data. Accounts with administrator access alone do not see it.

<Steps>
1. Open ① `학생 활성` (Student engagement) in the left-hand menu.
2. Under `기간` (Period), choose `전체` (All) or a year.
3. Under `비교 관점` (Comparison), choose `전공·비전공` (Major/non-major) or `학과` (Department).
</Steps>

## Read the numbers

- `가입 학생` (Registered students): The number of students who have registered.
- `활동 학생` (Active students): Students meeting the activity threshold, which is a total ranking score of at least 1.
- `프로그램 참여자` (Program participants): Students whose program participation has been approved.
- `활성 — 랭킹 지표` (Engagement — ranking metrics): Compares Commit, PR, Issue, Repo, Star (cumulative), and the total.
- `학과별 활성` (Engagement by department): Compares students and activity by department.
- `참여 — 프로그램별` (Participation — by program): Shows approved participation for each program.

Some vertical-axis numbers in the `활성 — 랭킹 지표` (Engagement — ranking metrics) chart may be clipped. Do not copy values from the axis alone; hover over a bar to check its value.

<Success>
Numbers and charts update for the selected period and comparison, with a summary line at the top such as `기간 전체 · 기준 …` (Period: all · As of …).
</Success>

<Recover title="If the screen is empty or cannot load">
`학과별 학생이 없습니다` (No students by department) and `승인된 참여가 없습니다` (No approved participation) mean there is nothing to aggregate for the selected period. Expand `기간` (Period) to `전체` (All). If you see `학생 활성을 불러오지 못했습니다` (Student engagement could not be loaded), refresh the page.
</Recover>
